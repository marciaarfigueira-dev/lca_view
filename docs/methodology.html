Field Emissions

Methane from flooded rice cultivation

Direct methane emissions are modelled as foreground inventory flows using the IPCC Tier 1 emission-factor approach for flooded rice cultivation. Because the available records do not provide consistent farmer-level differences in water management, methane is treated as a constant hectare-based emission across farmer-years. Differences in tonne-based methane impacts arise from farmer-year productivity.

Parameter Symbol Value Unit Use
Baseline emission factor EFc 1.56 kg CH4 ha-1 day-1 Central 2019 Refinement value. Sensitivity range: 1.06-2.31.
Water regime during cultivation SFw 0.60 - Common irrigated-rice assumption where farmer-specific flooding and drainage records were unavailable. Sensitivity alternatives: 0.55 and 1.00.
Pre-season water regime SFp 1.22 - Applied uniformly across farmer-years. Sensitivity alternatives: 1.00 and 2.41.
Organic amendment scaling SFo 1.00 - No straw methane multiplier in the central model because straw quantity and incorporation timing were not consistently recorded.
Cultivation duration t 130 days Common cultivation-period assumption. Sensitivity range: 111-153 days.
Seasonal methane CH4 148.45 kg CH4 ha-1 EFc x SFw x SFp x SFo x t.

Field Emissions

Nitrous oxide and urea CO2

Nitrous oxide emissions from managed soils include direct emissions from synthetic nitrogen, direct emissions from straw returned to the field, and indirect emissions from volatilisation and leaching/runoff. Synthetic nitrogen is separated by identifiable N form for volatilisation because the current workflow uses different volatilised fractions for urea, ammonium-based and unidentified fertiliser forms. Straw is included only as an emission-relevant residue input and is not treated as a co-product. Urea hydrolysis CO2 is added only for rows classified as urea.

Pathway Equation Constants and assumptions
Direct N2O from synthetic fertiliser F_SN x EF1FR x 44/28 EF1FR = 0.004 kg N2O-N kg-1 N for flooded rice. Sensitivity range: 0.003-0.005.
Direct N2O from crop residues F_CR x EF1FR x 44/28 F_CR = 40.5 kg N ha-1 in the central model; a no-residue-return scenario uses F_CR = 0.
Indirect N2O from volatilisation sum(F_SN,k x FracGASF_k) x EF4 x 44/28 EF4 = 0.010 kg N2O-N kg-1 volatilised N. Central FracGASF values: urea 0.15, ammonium-based 0.08, unknown N form 0.11.
Indirect N2O from leaching/runoff F_N x FracLEACH x EF5 x 44/28 FracLEACH = 0.24 and EF5 = 0.011 kg N2O-N kg-1 N lost in the central model.
CO2 from urea hydrolysis M_urea x EF_C x 44/12 EF_C = 0.20 kg C kg-1 urea. Mixed commercial fertilisers use reconstructed urea-equivalent mass where applicable.
Per tonne conversion Component per tonne = component per ha / productivity Productivity is farmer-year specific; this is why constant methane per hectare differs by tonne.
