How to read Graph interpretation Open the short guide before using the stacked graph.

The x-axis shows impact categories and each stacked colour shows the contribution from one input source. Use hectare basis to compare pressure on the field, and tonne basis to compare production efficiency.

Aggregation can be equal-weighted, area-weighted, production-weighted, or total burden. For one selected farmer-year, weighted means collapse to that farmer-year value; total burden scales the intensity by area or production.
