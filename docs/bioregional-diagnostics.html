PCA is treated as pattern detection and DEA as a technical-efficiency prototype. The sample contains 34 farmer-years from 12 farms, so frontier scores and clusters are sensitive to individual observations, model specification and missing contextual variables. The main article therefore relies on LCA per hectare, LCA per tonne and annual median benchmarks; this page preserves PCA/DEA only as a thesis-facing diagnostic path.
