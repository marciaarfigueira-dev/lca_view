Farmer-year benchmark methods

Explanation of the annual and pooled median benchmarks used to compare farmer-year management and LCA indicators within the observed Sado rice sample.

Methods note

Boundary

What this benchmark shows

Back to overview Back to benchmark

The benchmark positions each farmer-year relative to other observations in the same sample. It is descriptive, within-sample and non-causal. It does not classify farms as sustainable or unsustainable and does not estimate management effects.

References

Annual and pooled medians

The annual reference compares a farmer-year with the median of the same production year. The pooled reference compares it with the median across 2022-2024. Annual medians reduce direct comparison across different growing seasons; pooled medians show broader position within the three-year sample.

Calculation

Deviation from median

For each selected indicator, the page calculates percentage deviation from the relevant median: 100 x (farmer-year value minus median) divided by median. Positive values mean above the reference median and negative values mean below it. Whether above or below is favourable depends on the indicator.

Indicator Domains

What can be compared

Domain Indicators Interpretation
Productivity Yield and output-normalised indicators. Shows realised production context, not causal management success.
Nutrients N, P2O5 and K2O input indicators. Shows applied nutrient intensity, not crop uptake, surplus or losses.
Crop protection Active ingredient and crop-protection LCA indicators. Represents intervention intensity, not measured pest pressure.
Mechanisation Operated area and machinery LCA indicators. Represents standardised operation intensity, not measured fuel use.
Environmental profile EF 3.0 midpoint impacts per hectare and per tonne. Separates land-based pressure from output-normalised burden.
