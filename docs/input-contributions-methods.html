Methods note

Boundary

What this view shows

Back to overview Back to input graphs

Input-contribution graphs split characterised midpoint impacts by modelled source: sowing and seeds, fertiliser production, crop-protection production and field application, machinery operations, methane field emissions and nitrogen-related field emissions.

Calculation

Contribution shares

For each farmer-year, impact category and functional basis, the contribution of each input group is divided by the total modelled impact for that category. Stacked bars show mean shares because means preserve additivity in the visual display.

Input Groups

What the groups mean

Input group Contains Main limitation
Sowing and seeds Seed input and seed-production background burdens. On-farm seed handling and establishment success are not modelled.
Fertilisation Upstream N, P2O5 and K2O fertiliser production. Applied nutrients are not direct evidence of crop uptake or agronomic adequacy.
Crop protection Product-supply and generic field-emission processes scaled by active ingredient. Product-specific fate, exposure and treatment effectiveness are not represented.
Machinery Generic operation processes scaled by operated area. Measured fuel use, power, load, speed and field efficiency are unavailable.
Methane field emissions CH4 from flooded rice cultivation. Common per-hectare parameterisation because farm-specific water regime was unavailable.
Nitrogen-related field emissions Direct and indirect N2O and urea CO2 foreground flows. Generic emission factors, not measured field-specific losses.

Cautions

Dominant-driver interpretation

A dominant input group is interpreted cautiously where the leading source is close to the runner-up. Mean and median shares may differ for heterogeneous categories, especially freshwater ecotoxicity. Water-use contributions refer to background-process water use embedded in retained inputs, not measured on-farm irrigation.
