Method and limitations How indicators are linked

The benchmark links record-derived activity indicators to LCA results without treating those links as causal effects or compliance judgements. Irrigation records are retained as reporting evidence, but measured farmer-year water delivery and flooding dynamics were not available for the interpreted LCA. PCA and DEA are treated separately as exploratory method-development diagnostics, not as part of the farmer-year benchmark or as sustainability rankings.
