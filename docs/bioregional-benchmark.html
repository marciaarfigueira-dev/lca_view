Benchmarking framework

This table maps the activity indicator to the environmental result that should be benchmarked. It is a working setup for the final farmer-year comparison.

Dimension Activity or performance indicator Environmental result Scale Main interpretation
Productivity Yield, t/ha Full LCA profile per tonne Output-based Shows how realised yield mediates all environmental burdens per unit of rice.
Nitrogen management N input, kg N/ha; N surplus and NUE pending final assumptions Climate change, acidification, and eutrophication impacts Per hectare Land-based burdens associated with fertiliser production and nitrogen losses.
Nitrogen productivity N input, kg N/t rice Nitrogen-related LCA impacts per tonne Per tonne Nitrogen demand and associated burden relative to output.
Crop protection Active ingredient, kg/ha, disaggregated by product where possible Freshwater ecotoxicity and human-toxicity impacts Per hectare Chemical pressure and characterised toxicity associated with the pesticide portfolio.
Crop-protection productivity Active ingredient, kg/t rice Ecotoxicity and toxicity impacts per tonne Per tonne Chemical use and characterised toxicity relative to output.
Mechanisation Worked area or operations/ha Fossil-resource use, climate change, ionising radiation, and other machinery-related impacts Per hectare Land-based burdens associated with machinery operations.
Mechanisation productivity Worked area or operations/t rice Machinery-related impacts per tonne Per tonne Operational burden relative to output.
