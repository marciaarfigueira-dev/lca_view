Weather context methods

Explanation of the seasonal weather context layer used beside farmer-year LCA results. This page describes shared regional context, not farmer-level causal attribution.

Methods note

Boundary

How to read the weather layer

Back to overview Back to weather context

Weather indicators are shared Sado-region context variables for the rice-growing period. They help interpret whether the LCA years occurred under unusual seasonal conditions, but they do not explain individual farmer-year outcomes by themselves.

Baseline

Historical comparison basis

The weather page compares 2022-2024 against the Sado historical distribution for 1991-2024. Grey bands show Q1-Q3, the dark marker shows the historical median, and coloured points show the LCA years.

Metrics

Weather indicators shown

Metric group Indicators Interpretation
Temperature and heatwaves Mean daily maximum temperature, days above 35 deg C and 40 deg C, heatwave days above 35 deg C and 40 deg C. Thermal exposure and extreme-heat context during selected months.
VPD Mean vapour pressure deficit. Atmospheric drying demand, interpreted as regional context rather than field water status.
Precipitation Monthly precipitation. Rainfall context, not measured farm-level water availability or irrigation delivery.
NAO index Monthly North Atlantic Oscillation index. Large-scale atmospheric context, not a direct farm-level driver.

Yield Associations

Historical correlations

The yield-association table reports Pearson r, p-value and R2 between monthly context metrics and detrended regional yield residuals over 1991-2024. These are historical regional associations and should not be read as causal evidence for the 34 farmer-year LCA observations.
