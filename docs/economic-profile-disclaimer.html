Exploratory Method Development

Economic profiling boundary.

This view is an exploratory farmer-year cost-revenue profile. It is useful for method development and relative comparison, but it is not a complete farm-accounting or profitability assessment.

What is included

The profile compares paddy-rice revenue with selected input costs reconstructed from the available records.

Revenue

Paddy rice

Farmer-year revenue is estimated from harvested paddy-rice production and production-year paddy price scenarios.

Costs

Selected inputs

Costs currently include fertiliser products and crop-protection products only, using low, central and high price scenarios.

Margin

Partial margin

The displayed margin is revenue minus the selected priced input domains. It should be read as a partial indicator, not as farm profit.

What is excluded

Several important cost and income components are missing from this first exploratory layer.

Machinery

Ownership and operation

Machinery purchase, depreciation, maintenance, fuel, contractor fees and machine-specific operating costs are not included.

Farm costs

Labour and fixed costs

Labour, land rent, financing, insurance, storage, drying, transport, cooperative charges and post-harvest costs are outside the current calculation.

Policy

Subsidies and payments

CAP payments, coupled support, scheme payments and other income transfers are not included in the displayed margin.

Uncertainty

Prices are scenarios

Prices are screening assumptions rather than audited transaction prices paid or received by each farmer. The layer should be enhanced with verified prices and additional cost categories.
